My name is Hao Wu. You can call me Hao or by my English name Musan.

I am a final-year Ph.D. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), affiliated with the Institute of Software. I am honored to be advised by Prof. Naijun Zhan. I also completed my undergraduate studies in computer science at UCAS.

My research interests include:
- *Verification and Synthesis*: My goal is to create an extensible framework that combines symbolic and numerical methods to tackle bottleneck problems in the verification and synthesis of complex systems, such as programs, continuous-time dynamical systems, and control systems.
- *Theoretical Computer Science (TCS)*: I investigate foundational problems in logic, automata, complexity, and computability to gain a deeper understanding of these core concepts.
In one sentence, I study the system of systems and the logic of logics.
